# Customer Support Response Framework
**Project type:** Practice portfolio project  
**Role demonstrated:** Customer Support / Customer Experience

## Scenario
A customer reports that they cannot sign in to an application and is worried because they need it for work.

## Sample response
“Hi, thanks for letting us know. I understand how frustrating it is when you need to use the application and cannot sign in. I’ll help you work through the next steps. Could you tell me the exact error message you see and whether other websites are opening normally? Please do not send me your password. I’ll use the details you share to check the appropriate troubleshooting steps. If this needs a specialist, I’ll document what we’ve tried and route it to the right team. I’ll explain the next step clearly before we close this conversation.”

## Principles demonstrated
- Acknowledge the concern without making promises that cannot be guaranteed.
- Ask focused questions to identify the issue.
- Protect private information.
- Explain the next step and escalation path.
- Close only when the issue is resolved or the customer understands what happens next.

## Quality checklist
- Was the customer acknowledged?
- Were questions relevant and easy to answer?
- Were privacy and security protected?
- Were next steps and expectations clear?
