# Help Desk Ticket & Escalation Template
**Project type:** Practice portfolio project  
**Role demonstrated:** IT Support / Customer Support

## Ticket fields
- **Ticket ID:** [ID]
- **Date/time reported:** [Date and time with time zone]
- **Requester:** [Name or internal identifier]
- **Category:** [Account / Network / Hardware / Software / Other]
- **Priority:** [Based on business impact and urgency]
- **Affected service/device:** [Details]
- **Issue summary:** [One sentence]
- **User impact:** [What the person cannot do; number of users affected]
- **Steps already tried:** [List steps and results]
- **Error message:** [Exact wording, if available]
- **Resolution or next action:** [Clear action]
- **Status:** [Open / In progress / Pending / Resolved]
- **Escalation details:** [Team, reason, evidence, and time escalated]

## Example (fictional)
**Issue:** User cannot sign in to a work application.  
**Impact:** One user cannot access the application needed for their task.  
**Checks:** Confirmed internet access; asked user to verify the correct sign-in page; captured the exact error text.  
**Next action:** Follow the approved account recovery process. If the account remains locked, route to the identity/access team.  
**Communication:** “I’ve confirmed your internet connection is working and captured the sign-in error. I’m checking the next approved account-recovery step. I’ll update you when I have a confirmed next action.”

## Good ticket habits
- Use factual, respectful language.
- Do not record passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
- Separate confirmed facts from assumptions.
- Make ownership and next steps clear.
