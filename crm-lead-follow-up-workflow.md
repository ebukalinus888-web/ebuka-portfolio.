# CRM Lead Follow-up Workflow
**Project type:** Practice portfolio project  
**Role demonstrated:** Sales Development / Business Development

## Goal
Keep prospect communication organized and ensure each qualified conversation has a clear next action.

## Sample stages
1. New lead
2. Contact attempted
3. Connected / discovery
4. Qualified
5. Follow-up scheduled
6. Proposal or next step
7. Won / lost / nurture

## Example CRM record (fictional)
- **Company:** Northstar Office Supplies
- **Contact role:** Operations Manager
- **Need:** Interested in improving how enquiries are tracked
- **Last interaction:** Introductory call
- **Status:** Follow-up scheduled
- **Next action:** Send a concise summary and ask whether a 15-minute discovery call next week is suitable
- **Follow-up date:** [Add date]
- **Notes:** Confirm current process and decision criteria before recommending a solution

## Follow-up message
“Hello, thanks for speaking with me. From our conversation, it sounds like keeping track of incoming enquiries is an area you want to improve. Would a short discovery call next week be useful so I can understand your current process and see whether we may be able to help?”

## Good CRM practice
- Record only relevant, professional information.
- Use accurate statuses and realistic follow-up dates.
- Respect opt-outs and communication preferences.
- Avoid claiming a lead is qualified until the agreed criteria are met.
